About PNG to JPG Conversion

PNG is a lossless image format with transparency support, while JPG is a lossy format that provides better compression. Converting PNG to JPG is useful when you need smaller file sizes. Note that JPG doesn't support transparency, so transparent areas will be filled with white background.
